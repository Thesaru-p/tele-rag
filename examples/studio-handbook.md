# The Sunday Studio — customer handbook

The Sunday Studio offers beginner pottery classes and private group sessions.

## Opening hours

We open Tuesday through Sunday, 9 am to 6 pm, and close on Mondays.

## Beginner pottery class

The beginner class starts at 10 am on Sundays and lasts two hours. The price is $35 per person. Clay, tools, glazing, and firing are included. No experience is required.

## Private groups

Private group sessions start at $180 for up to six people. Contact the studio to arrange a date.

## What to bring

Wear clothes you do not mind getting messy. Aprons and tools are provided.

## Cancellation

Cancel at least 48 hours before class for a full refund. This handbook does not specify a refund for later cancellations.
