04 / THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A few things before you get started.

What does TeleRAG actually do?

TeleRAG turns your business documents into a searchable knowledge base. Ask questions in your workspace or connect a Telegram bot so customers can get answers grounded in your files, with source references.

What can I upload?

PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT, and Markdown files up to 10 MiB each. Use documents with selectable text; scanned documents, image OCR, and Excel workbooks aren’t supported yet. Split longer documents into smaller files; each upload supports up to 160 searchable passages.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. The interactive demo above runs in your browser with three sample documents from a fictional pottery studio. Create an account in the workspace to upload and ask questions about your own files.

How do I connect Telegram?

Create a bot with Telegram’s BotFather, then paste its token into the Telegram section of your signed-in workspace. A public HTTPS deployment is needed so Telegram can deliver messages to your bot.

Where do my documents go?

Files are stored in private Supabase storage. Extracted text is processed through OpenRouter for indexing and answers. Each business’s retrieval is scoped to its own documents.
