      Your bot sends this when someone presses Start. To also show it before Start, open BotFather ↗, send /setdescription, select your bot, and paste this paragraph.

      Need to create a bot? Follow these steps
      1. Open BotFather on Telegram ↗.
      2. Send /newbot, then choose a display name and a username ending in bot.
      3. Copy the token BotFather gives you, return here, and paste it above.

      Keep the token private. Anyone who has it can control your bot. If it leaks, revoke it with BotFather and connect the replacement.
